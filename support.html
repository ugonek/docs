Where it goes

Tips help cover the small ongoing costs of running the bot and the time spent adding new features, tracking down curated achievement tips, and keeping things working as Steam and Discord change their APIs.

Other ways to help

  • Use /wanted-games-adjacent flows in the bot — clicking "Request tracking for this game" tells the developer which games are worth prioritizing next, at no cost to you.
  • Report bugs or oddities you run into — feedback is as valuable as a tip.
  • Tell a friend who'd find it useful.
Tipping is entirely voluntary and does not unlock any feature, priority support, or special treatment — every user gets the same bot either way. See the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for how the bot itself works.
