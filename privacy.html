Privacy Policy

Achievement Goblin — Discord Bot
Effective date: August 23, 2026

← Back  ·  Terms of Service

This Privacy Policy explains what information Achievement Goblin (the "Bot") collects when you use it, why it's collected, how it's stored, and how you can have it removed. It applies to anyone who links a Steam account to the Bot or otherwise interacts with it on Discord.

The Bot is developed and operated by an individual (the "Operator"), not a company. You can reach the Operator at ugonek+goblin@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

1. Information the Bot Collects

DataWhere it comes fromWhy it's collected
Discord user ID Provided automatically by Discord when you interact with the Bot Identifies your account so the Bot knows which Steam link, settings, and history belong to you
Discord presence data (the game you're currently shown as playing) Discord's presence system, only for users the Bot can see presence for Lets the Bot proactively detect what you're playing and offer to check achievements, without you having to type a command
Steam ID (SteamID64) Entered by you via the Bot's link command Required to query Valve's Steam Web API for your game and achievement data
Steam achievement & game data (which achievements exist for a game, which ones you've unlocked) Retrieved live from Valve's public Steam Web API using your linked Steam ID Core functionality — showing missing achievements, progress percentages, comparisons, and leaderboards
Game history (which games you've checked and when) Generated by the Bot as you use commands Powers the progress overview and lets the Bot fall back to "the game you last checked" when live presence isn't available
Preferences (notification, comparison, leaderboard, and hidden-achievement settings) Set by you via the Bot's settings command Respects your choices about what the Bot does and what it shares with others
Game requests ("please add tip data for this game") Generated when you click the Bot's request-tracking button Lets the Operator prioritize which games to add curated achievement tips for

The Bot does not collect or read the content of your Discord messages. It does not request Discord's message-content permission and has no ability to see what is typed in server channels.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to operate the Bot's features: detecting the game you're playing, retrieving and displaying your Steam achievement progress, computing completion percentages, running comparisons and leaderboards you and other users have opted into, remembering your settings, and tracking which games have been requested for tip coverage. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features, and it is not sold.

3. Where Data Is Stored

The Bot stores your Discord ID, linked Steam ID, preferences, game history, and game-request records in a local database on the server the Operator runs. It is not uploaded to a third-party analytics or marketing platform. Achievement and game data itself is fetched from Steam on demand rather than permanently duplicated in bulk.

4. Who Data Is Shared With

Two third parties necessarily see limited data as part of how the Bot works:

  • Discord Inc. — processes your messages, interactions, and presence data as part of operating the Discord platform itself, under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Valve Corporation (Steam) — receives your SteamID64 in API requests when the Bot looks up your game and achievement data, under Valve's own privacy practices.

Other Discord users only see information you've opted to share: comparison results are only computed with users who also have comparisons enabled, and leaderboard entries only include members who've opted into a given server's leaderboard. Beyond Discord and Valve, we do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell it.

5. Your Choices

  • Toggle presence notifications, friend comparisons, leaderboard participation, and hidden-achievement display at any time via the Bot's settings command.
  • Stop using the Bot at any time by not interacting with it, blocking its DMs, or having it removed from a server.
  • Making your Steam "Game details" private, or setting your Steam status to Invisible, will stop the Bot from being able to detect or retrieve your game data (this also affects functionality, since it's how the Bot works).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your linked data is retained for as long as your Steam account remains linked, so the Bot can continue to function for you. You can request deletion of all data associated with your Discord ID at any time by emailing ugonek+goblin@gmail.com. The Operator will remove your linked Steam ID, preferences, game history, and game-request records within a reasonable time (target: 30 days) of a verified request.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children and follows Discord's own minimum age requirements for platform use. We do not knowingly collect data from users who do not meet Discord's minimum age.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or other privacy concerns can be sent to ugonek+goblin@gmail.com.
